brush kit for bicycle

-

[lubricant]

note: of course, a mask is mandatory for this kind of activity

recommended brands: oxford and wd - 40

phase 1 - position the bike in a clean and spacious space

step 1 = fasten bicycle thanks to bike rack for a better control + remove all stuffs non-essentials from bike (gps, pannier, rear seat for a second person, lamp…)

If the bicycle hasn't cleaned for so long, or just too muddy :

step 1.5 = to ensure the parts are visible, rinse using 1 sponge with few water

note: be serious especially to the motor on the bike (wheels/spokes, hub [center of the wheel], chain, rear sprockets [rear gears], rear derailleur [what holds the chain at the bottom at the rear], chainring [gears that hold the pedals])

phase 2 - remove max greases (muds, black stains, dusts)

step 2 = without scrub hard but very well :

  • the round/soft brush or (more efficient) the degreasing case for the bike chain
  • the compressed air can and the bike grease-removing (or wd - 40 unjam) for the rest of the motor (see note above)

step 3 =

1 - wait 5 minutes (or more if high black deposits)

2 - rinse each trace of grease thanks to another sponge full water

phase 3 - let's clean!

step 4 = scrub thanks to (+ bike spray) :

the hard brush
_ chainring
the round brush
_ hub
the paintbrush
_ rear sprockets
_ brake calipers [area where the wheel brakes exactly]
_ inaccessible zones
new sponge
_ chain
_ frame [bicycle's body]
_ chainring
_ wheels / spokes
_ accessories

to take seriously in the future… otherwise, serious consequences will fall !
